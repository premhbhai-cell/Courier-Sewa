How It Works

Five Steps, One Trail of Data.

Each step reuses what the last one captured — nothing is entered twice, and nothing gets billed by memory.

1

Book

Pick the client, fill sender and receiver, weight and dimensions. Rates apply themselves.

2

Label

AWB number and barcode generate instantly; print the consignment note and stick it on.

3

Move

Assign delivery staff, update status at each hop. Customers follow it on public tracking.

4

Deliver

Capture proof of delivery against the AWB. The delivery date stamps itself.

5

Bill

Pull the period's bookings into a GST-ready invoice, record payment, close the ledger.
