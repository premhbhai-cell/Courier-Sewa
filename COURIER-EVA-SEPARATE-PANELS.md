# Courier Eva — Separate Role Panels

Each role has a dedicated entry URL and role-specific landing panel.

- Super Admin: dashboard/panels/super-admin.html
- Admin: dashboard/panels/admin.html
- Branch Admin: dashboard/panels/branch-admin.html
- Staff: dashboard/panels/staff.html
- Collection Boy: dashboard/panels/collection-boy.html
- Delivery Portal: dashboard/panels/delivery.html
- Staff Management: dashboard/pages/panel-staff-management.html (management-access page)

The login page routes the selected role to its dedicated panel. The header shows the active panel name and role. Shared operational modules remain linked from each role's own panel according to its permissions.
